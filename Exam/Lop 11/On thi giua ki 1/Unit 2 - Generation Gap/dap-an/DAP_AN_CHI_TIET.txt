Câu 44: [Đáp án đúng: B]
Nội dung: a. Son: Dad, I want to learn to fix my bike instead of buying a new one.b. Dad: Good idea; repairing teaches patience and saves money.c. Son: Can we start with the brakes? They feel loose.d. Dad: We’ll watch a tutorial first, then try it together.e. Son: Thanks! I’ll get the tools ready after dinner.
      A. a – c – b – d – e
  (*) B. a – b – c – d – e
      C. b – a – d – c – e
      D. a – b – d – c – e
Giải thích: Thứ tự hội thoại: a (Con muốn tự sửa xe) ➔ b (Bố khen ý hay) ➔ c (Con hỏi bắt đầu từ phanh) ➔ d (Bố bảo xem hướng dẫn trước) ➔ e (Con cảm ơn và chuẩn bị dụng cụ). Đáp án B: a – b – c – d – e.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Câu 45: [Đáp án đúng: B]

Families that combine modern skills with traditional wisdom build stronger connections.c. For example, a teenager might teach photo editing while learning a folk dance in return.d. This exchange, which respects each person’s strengths, reduces conflict and builds trust.e. Consequently, both generations feel useful and proud of what they contribute.
      A. c – b – a – e – d
  (*) B. b – c – a – d – e
      C. b – a – c – d – e
