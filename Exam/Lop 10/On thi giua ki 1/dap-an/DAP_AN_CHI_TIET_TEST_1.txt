[PHẦN: II. READING - PART 1: CLOZE TEST]
Tiêu đề: Working Mothers in the Modern Family
Hướng dẫn: Read the following passage carefully and then choose the best option to fit each numbered blank (from 11 to 15).
Áp dụng cho các câu hỏi: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15

--- VĂN BẢN ĐOẠN VĂN ---
In the United States today, more than half of mothers with young children work, compared to about one third in the 1970s. Women have been moving into the workforce not only for career satisfaction (11) ______ for the income.

In many families today, mothers continue to work because they have careers that they have spent years developing. Some women return to work soon after giving birth because they know that most employers are not sympathetic to working mothers (12) ______ wish to take time off to be with their young children.

Some people still think that a “good mother” is one who gives (13) ______ work to stay home with her children. However, no scientific evidence says children are harmed when their mothers work. A child who is emotionally well adjusted, well loved, and well cared for will thrive regardless of whether the mother works outside the home.

In most families with working mothers, each person (14) ______ a more active role in the household. The children tend to look after one another and help in other ways. The father as a breadwinner is more likely to help with household chores and child rearing. These positive outcomes are most likely when the working mother feels valued and (15) ______ by family, friends, and co-workers.
(Adapted from giainhanh.vn)

[PHẦN: II. READING - PART 2: READING COMPREHENSION]
Tiêu đề: The Value of Household Chores for Children
Hướng dẫn: Read the following passage and mark the letter A, B, C, or D on your answer sheet to indicate the correct answer to each of the questions.
Áp dụng cho các câu hỏi: 16, 17, 18, 19, 20

--- VĂN BẢN ĐOẠN VĂN ---
Chores are not only housework, they also help children grow. When kids do chores, they learn important life skills and feel good about themselves. Helping at home shows them that their work has value and that they are part of the family. This gives them more <u>confidence</u>.

Doing chores also teaches children how to plan, organize, and finish tasks. For example, setting the table or sorting clothes helps <u>them</u> practice following steps and managing time. Chores also train kids to wait for rewards. Instead of playing right away, they finish cleaning and then enjoy a tidy room. This builds self-control and patience.

Chores are also good for mental health. When children see the result of their work, they feel proud and happy. They understand that their help makes a difference. In the long run, chores prepare kids for the future. They learn how to cook, clean, and take care of themselves, which makes them ready for independent life as adults.

Parents can start with simple jobs for toddlers, like putting toys away, and give harder tasks as kids grow. It is important to give praise, focus on effort, and make chores fun. With this support, chores become a way to build confidence, responsibility, and independence.
(Adapted from Brighthorizons.com)

      B. laundry

Giải thích: routine có trọng âm rơi vào âm tiết thứ 2 (/ruːˈtiːn/), các từ còn lại có trọng âm rơi vào âm tiết thứ nhất: laundry (/ˈlɔːndri/), household (/ˈhaʊshəʊld/), picnic (/ˈpɪknɪk/).

Câu 3: [Đáp án đúng: A]
Nội dung: Jim _____________ a book at the moment.
  (*) A. is reading
      B. had read
      C. reads
      D. has read
Giải thích: Dấu hiệu thời gian "at the moment" (ngay lúc này) chỉ hành động đang diễn ra tại thời điểm nói -> dùng thì Hiện tại tiếp diễn: S + am/is/are + V-ing (Jim is reading).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Câu 4: [Đáp án đúng: D]
Nội dung: Bich sometimes ____________chicken noodle soup for breakfast.
      A. are having
      B. is having
      C. have
  (*) D. has
Giải thích: Trạng từ tần suất "sometimes" diễn tả thói quen lặp đi lặp lại -> thì Hiện tại đơn. Chủ ngữ "Bich" là ngôi thứ 3 số ít nên động từ have chia thành has.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Câu 5: [Đáp án đúng: B]
Nội dung: My mother____________the responsibility for running the household.
      A. holds
  (*) B. takes
      C. runs
      D. bears
Giải thích: Cụm từ cố định (collocation): "take the responsibility for (doing) sth" = chịu trách nhiệm làm việc gì (quán xuyến gia đình).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Câu 6: [Đáp án đúng: D]
Nội dung: I'm lucky to have such a(n)__________ brother who always gives me help when I need it.
      A. support
      B. Supporting
      C. supported
  (*) D. supportive
Giải thích: Vị trí cần điền đứng trước danh từ "brother" cần một tính từ miêu tả tính cách. "supportive" (adj) mang nghĩa luôn sẵn lòng hỗ trợ, ủng hộ người khác.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Câu 7: [Đáp án đúng: A]
Nội dung: Doing chores as a family will help strengthen ____________.
  (*) A. family bonds
      B. generation gap
      C. family trees
      D. family values
Giải thích: Collocation trọng tâm Unit 1: "strengthen family bonds" = thắt chặt / gắn kết tình cảm gia đình. (generation gap: khoảng cách thế hệ; family trees: phả hệ; family values: giá trị gia đình).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Câu 8: [Đáp án đúng: A]
Nội dung: My eldest son is strong enough to do the___________ for the family.
  (*) A. heavy lifting
      B. household chores
      C. cooking
      D. laundry
Giải thích: Collocation: "do the heavy lifting" = làm công việc nặng nhọc đòi hỏi thể lực. Cụm "strong enough" (đủ khoẻ) bổ trợ chỉ việc mang vác nặng.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Câu 9: [Đáp án đúng: B]
Nội dung: Mark the letter A, B, C, or D to indicate the word(s) CLOSEST in meaning to the underlined word(s): <u>Domestic</u> chores will no longer be a burden thanks to the inventions of laborsaving devices.

      C. Foreign
